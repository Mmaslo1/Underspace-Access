=====================================================================
PART 1. WHAT KIND OF GAME THIS IS
=====================================================================

- Underspace is an open-world space game. You are a "starchaser": a freelance pilot with your own small ship.
- You fly between space stations and planets. You take jobs, fight pirates and space monsters,
  collect loot, trade cargo, buy better weapons and ships, and level up.
- The universe is split into star systems. Inside a system you travel by:
  - Flying yourself (slow).
  - The autopilot: you pick a target and the ship flies there on its own. Sighted players use it all the time.
  - Lanelines: highways made of rings. You fly into the first ring and the laneline carries you at high speed
    to the far end. Pirates sometimes ambush lanelines.
- To get to another star system you go through a jumpgate or a jumphole.
- There is no "fast travel" button. Trains exist later (you pay a captain to carry you to stations you have found).

GAME MODES
- Campaign: the story, with tutorial missions (navigation, combat, docking).
- Freeplay: what you are playing. No story, no tutorial. You start at a station and do whatever you like.
  We stay in Freeplay for now, and do the Campaign later.

PART 3. WHAT TO DO FIRST IN FREEPLAY (my suggested plan)
=====================================================================

1. In the station press F3 (warp to the bar). Then press Z: the Job Board opens. No walking needed.
   Read the jobs with the arrows. Take an EASY one: a low difficulty number, near your level (you are 5).
   The job gives you instructions and usually a waypoint (a marker the mod puts first in your space list).
2. Press F2 (warp to the launch bay). Then press Z: your ship launches into space.
3. In space: Page Down. The first thing in the list is your waypoint, if the job gave one.
   Press Enter to select it, then F2: the autopilot flies there.
4. Do the job (often: fight a few pirates). Collect the loot with B.
5. Come back to a station: select it, press F2, the autopilot flies there and docks.
6. Spend the money on better weapons and shields at the Equipment Dealer (F4 in the station).
7. Repeat. Your level goes up with money, loot and items. Level-ups give skill points (I = character sheet).

PART 5. IN SPACE: HOW TO FLY (new in mod version 0.8.0)
=====================================================================

THE IDEA
- Sighted players see nearby objects in a "contact list" in the lower left corner and click one to target it.
- The mod now reads that same list to you and selects things for you the same way a click does.
- Then you let the game's own autopilot fly there (F2), or you steer yourself.

TWO KINDS OF LISTS IN SPACE
- NEARBY (what your scanner sees, a few kilometers around you): categories Important, Ships, Solars,
  Wreckage, Loot, All. This is why you only found 2 stations: only things close by are in these.
- THE MAP (new in 0.9.0): the last two categories.
  - "Map, places in this system": every known station, planet, jumpgate and laneline in the whole
    system, at any distance. Stations and planets first, then jumpgates, then lanelines.
  - "Map, ways to other star systems": every jumpgate and jumphole in this system, and which system it
    leads to. You travel one system at a time: go through a gate, then in the new system pick the next gate.
- Shift+Page Down / Shift+Page Up moves between all 8 categories.

STEP BY STEP: FLY TO A STATION AND DOCK (proven in your 7:14 AM test)
1. Pick the station (Solars category or the map). Enter: "Target: ...".
2. F2: the autopilot flies there AND docks: you hear "docking", "entering the dock", "docked".
   (If it only flies close and stops, press F2 or F once more.)
3. Once docked, the game loads the station inside and you are walking again. Nothing else to press.

STEP BY STEP: TRAVEL ANYWHERE WITH THE MAP
1. Shift+Page Down until "Map, places in this system". Page Down to the place you want. Enter.
   You hear "Route set to ...". The game plans the fastest way, using lanelines.
2. F3: the autopilot follows the route (the game's auto-follow). You hear "Following the route" and
   "Next waypoint: ..." at each step. Lanelines carry you at high speed.
   (F2 instead flies straight there: slower, but simple.)
3. When you arrive at a station or planet, press F to dock.

STEP BY STEP: GO TO ANOTHER STAR SYSTEM
1. Shift+Page Down until "Map, ways to other star systems". Page Down: "To Clearhome, through
   Omega-Alpha Jumpgate, 9 kilometers" (example). Enter: route to that gate.
2. F2 (fly straight to the gate) or F3 (follow the route).
3. At the gate the autopilot flies you in and you jump (if it stops near the gate, press F2 or F).
   You hear "Entering <system>".
4. In the new system, the map categories show its stations, planets and gates. Pick the next gate.
- Why not "fly to Clearhome" in one go: the game only plans routes through gates you have already
  discovered, and you have not discovered any yet. That is why it said "No path available".

MANUAL DOCKING (important)
- L switches manual docking on and off. The game says "Manual docking enabled" or "disabled".
- Keep it DISABLED: then the autopilot docks for you. In your 7:10 test you pressed L, the game said
  "Manual docking disabled", and after that docking worked.
- If you hear "Manual docking enabled", press L once more.

OTHER KEYS
- F1 cancels the autopilot. You hear "Autopilot off, you are flying."
- Pressing F2 again while it flies also stops it (the key switches on and off).
- End: at any time, where is my target: distance, direction, and how many degrees left/right, up/down.
- F10: ship status: hull, shields, energy, speed, throttle, autopilot, target.

LANELINES (space highways)
- Select a laneline (Solars category) and press F2. The ship flies into the ring and the laneline carries you.
- The mod says "travelling on a laneline", then "leaving the laneline" at the end.
- Escape leaves a laneline early (NOT CHECKED YET: the game's tip says the "close menu" key does it).
- You cannot use weapons on a laneline.

FLYING BY HAND
- W: more throttle (speed). S: less. The mod says the throttle when you let go.
- Double tap W: high engines (much faster; guns are off while they run). The mod says on and off.
- X: reverse. Left Shift: thrust (afterburner, a short burst).
- Steering: the game steers with the mouse. The mod adds keys:
  - Hold Left / Right arrow: turn the nose left / right.
  - Hold Up / Down arrow: nose up / down.
  - Hold Shift too: turn slowly, for fine aiming.
  - When you let go, you hear where your target is now.
- Q / E: roll (tilt the ship). A / D: slide sideways. Double tap A or D: dodge.
- Space: mouse follow on/off (the game's "free flight" switch). If the ship keeps turning by itself when you
  are not pressing anything, the mouse is steering: press Space once to switch mouse steering off.

PART 5B. DOCKING: WHAT WENT WRONG AT THE AXLE, AND THE SIMPLE WAY
=====================================================================

What happened (from your log):

- So the only problem was MANUAL DOCKING. When it is ENABLED, the station allows you in, but the game
  expects you to steer into the dock yourself. When it is DISABLED, the autopilot flies you in.
- The game remembers this setting, and L flips it each time you press it. Pressing L twice puts it back.

The simple way, every time:
1. Select the station. Press F2. Wait until the autopilot stops near it.
2. Press F. You should hear "Dock Request Granted", then "Autopilot: entering the dock", then "docked".
3. NEW: if the ship does not fly in, the mod now says: "Docking was granted, but the ship does not fly in
   by itself: manual docking is on. Press L once..." Press L ONCE, wait for "Manual docking disabled",
   then F again.
4. NEW: if you press F2 when you are already close, the mod says "You are already close. Press F to dock."

DOCKING: WAIT, DO NOT PRESS KEYS (new in 0.9.3, from your October 27 test)
- On October 27 your docking at The Axle actually WORKED: you heard "docked". But one moment later F3
  was pressed, and that took the ship out of the dock again. The second time, Enter was pressed while
  the ship was entering the dock, and it got stuck.
- Entering the dock takes about 15 seconds. NEW: the mod now says "Entering the dock. This takes about

- If it takes longer than 45 seconds, the mod tells you: F1 cancels, then choose the station and F2 again.

WHY THE SHIP IS SLOW (the "snail")

- For long trips, use the route instead: on a map place press Enter, then F3. The route uses lanelines
  and high engines (in your 7:10 test it reached 309 speed, about 3 times faster).
- While flying yourself you can double tap W for high engines.

PART 5C. TRADING COMMODITIES (buy cheap, sell expensive)
=====================================================================

The idea:
- Each station sells some goods (commodities) and buys others. Prices differ between stations.
- You buy goods where they are cheap, fly them to a station that pays more, and sell them.
- Your ship has a cargo hold. Your Shoreline holds 15 units ("0 of 15 used" means empty).
- Your level goes up with your net worth, so trading also levels you up. There are no separate points for trading.
- From mod version 0.9.4, every buy or sale says what you traded, the price, profit or loss, credits, hold, level and net worth.
- Careful: some stations scan your cargo ("contraband scan"). Do not carry illegal goods.

How to do it (with mod version 0.9.2):
1. In a station, warp to the cargo trader room. Press Z: the trade window opens.
   NEW: the mod says your credits and your cargo hold, for example "Credits 2,000. Cargo hold 0 of 15 used."
2. The first buttons are categories: "Commodities" is the goods for trading. Press Enter on it.
3. Up / Down arrows: the goods. NEW: each one says its name, price each, how many for sale, and its
   category, for example "Consumer Goods, $19 each, 200 for sale, Commodity, X buys one, A buys all".
4. X buys one. A buys as many as fit in your hold (or as many as you can pay for).
   FIXED in 0.9.3: on October 27 X and A did nothing, because the game only listens to them when the
   real mouse pointer is on the goods. Now the mod does the buying itself. You hear for example
   "Bought 1 Consumer Goods. Credits $14,981. Cargo hold 1 of 15 used."
   If it cannot buy, you hear why (hold full, not enough credits).
   Enter on a goods opens the game's own amount window. NEW: the mod reads it (price, amount, total).
   In it, Left and Right arrows on the slider change the amount, then the Buy button. Escape closes it.
5. WHERE DO I SELL IT? Press F11 on a goods. The mod asks the game's best-price list and says
   "To earn money, sell it at: <station> <price>", and where it is cheapest to buy.
   - Important: the game only knows prices of stations you have docked at and opened the cargo
     trader. At the start the list is empty, and the mod says so.
   - So: visit 2 or 3 stations (The Axle, Spokes Waste Depot, Communication Relay are all in this
     system), open the cargo trader at each one (Z), then press F11 again.
   - Ijuni trademasters on capital planets and big stations tell base prices of most goods.
6. Escape closes the window.
7. Fly to a station that pays more, open its cargo trader with Z, find your goods (they show
   "you have 15"), and press A to sell all. X sells one.

A good first trade:
- Buy one kind of goods at The Axle, sell it at a station in the next system, and see the difference.
- Start small: buy a few with X, so a bad price does not hurt.

PART 5D. HOW DO I KNOW WHERE TO GO?
=====================================================================

- Quests, jobs and bounties tell you: they set a waypoint, and it comes first in the Important list.
  Then Page Down, Enter, F3 (or F2).
- "Map, places in this system": every station, planet and gate of the system you are in.

    "No known route yet" until you have explored the way. Go gate by gate: each jump teaches the game
    a new connection, and later routes get longer and easier.
- The system description also mentions dangers, factions and what is there. Listen to it before you jump.
- Bars and the News Board (C in the bar) often mention places worth visiting.
- On a station, F10 says where you are. In space, F10 says your ship status.

PART 6. COMBAT
=====================================================================

- The mod warns you: "Enemy: <name>, 2.1 kilometers, ahead left."
- Comma: the game targets the nearest enemy. The mod says its name.
- Hold Home: the mod turns your nose onto the target. "On target" means your guns point at it.
- Right mouse button: fire your guns. (You can change this to a keyboard key in Options, Controls.)
- Keep holding Home while you fire, so you stay on target as it moves.
- End: target distance, direction, and its hull and shields in percent.
- Damage warnings: "Shields 50 percent", "Shields down!", "Hull 40 percent!".
- G: recharge shields. Hold G: repair the hull (uses repair items you carry).
- Double tap A or D: dodge sideways.
- C: countermeasures (against missiles). T: heavy equipment (if you have it). 1 to 0: use equipment slots.
- When the enemy dies: "<name> destroyed. If it dropped cargo, press B to collect it."
- Running away: double tap W for high engines.
- If your ship is destroyed the game offers respawn or reload; the mod reads that screen.
